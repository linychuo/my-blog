## 25条实用英语语法规则

### 1. LESS vs FEWER
- **用法**：less 修饰不可数名词，fewer 修饰可数名词
- **例句**：
  - There is **less** water in the bottle.
  - There are **fewer** people in the room.

### 2. AFFECT vs EFFECT
- **用法**：affect 作动词（影响），effect 作名词（效果）
- **例句**：
  - The weather **affects** my mood.
  - The **effect** of the storm was severe.

### 3. WHO vs WHOM
- **用法**：who 作主语，whom 作宾语
- **例句**：
  - **Who** called you?
  - To **whom** did you speak?

### 4. LAY vs LIE
- **用法**：lay 需要宾语（放置），lie 无需宾语（躺下）
- **例句**：
  - She **lays** the book on the table.
  - He **lies** down to rest.

### 5. YOUR vs YOU'RE
- **用法**：your 是形容词性物主代词，you're = you are
- **例句**：
  - Is this **your** pen?
  - **You're** late for the meeting.

### 6. ITS vs IT'S
- **用法**：its 是形容词性物主代词，it's = it is
- **例句**：
  - The cat licked **its** paw.
  - **It's** raining outside.

### 7. THEN vs THAN
- **用法**：then 表示时间顺序，than 用于比较
- **例句**：
  - First we eat, **then** we go.
  - She is taller **than** her brother.

### 8. THERE vs THEIR vs THEY'RE
- **用法**：
  - there = 那里
  - their = 他们的
  - they're = they are
- **例句**：
  - **There** is a book on the table.
  - **Their** house is big.
  - **They're** coming soon.

### 9. WHO'S vs WHOSE
- **用法**：who's = who is，whose = 谁的
- **例句**：
  - **Who's** at the door?
  - **Whose** book is this?

### 10. ALOT vs A LOT
- **用法**：alot 是错误拼写，正确为 a lot
- **例句**：
  - She has **a lot** of friends.

### 11. I.E. vs E.G.
- **用法**：
  - i.e. = that is（即）
  - e.g. = for example（例如）
- **例句**：
  - I need to buy groceries, **i.e.**, food and drinks.
  - I like fruits, **e.g.**, apples and bananas.

### 12. PRINCIPAL vs PRINCIPLE
- **用法**：
  - principal = 校长/主要的
  - principle = 原则
- **例句**：
  - The **principal** gave a speech.
  - Honesty is a core **principle**.

### 13. LOOSE vs LOSE
- **用法**：
  - loose = 松的
  - lose = 丢失
- **例句**：
  - This shirt is too **loose**.
  - Don't **lose** your keys.

### 14. ADVICE vs ADVISE
- **用法**：
  - advice = 名词（建议）
  - advise = 动词（建议）
- **例句**：
  - She gave me good **advice**.
  - I **advise** you to study more.

### 15. COMPLIMENT vs COMPLEMENT
- **用法**：
  - compliment = 赞美
  - complement = 补充
- **例句**：
  - He gave her a **compliment**.
  - The wine **complements** the meal.

### 16. DISCREET vs DISCRETE
- **用法**：
  - discreet = 低调的
  - discrete = 离散的
- **例句**：
  - Be **discreet** with the information.
  - These are **discrete** units.

### 17. EMIGRATE vs IMMIGRATE
- **用法**：
  - emigrate = 离开某国
  - immigrate = 进入某国
- **例句**：
  - She **emigrated** from China.
  - He **immigrated** to Canada.

### 18. LEND vs BORROW
- **用法**：
  - lend = 借出
  - borrow = 借入
- **例句**：
  - Can you **lend** me a pen?
  - I **borrowed** a book from the library.

### 19. ALREADY vs ALL READY
- **用法**：
  - already = 已经
  - all ready = 全部准备好
- **例句**：
  - I've **already** eaten.
  - We are **all ready** to go.

### 20. BRING vs TAKE
- **用法**：
  - bring = 带来（向说话者方向）
  - take = 带走（离开说话者方向）
- **例句**：
  - Please **bring** your notebook.
  - **Take** this book to the library.

### 21. FURTHER vs FARTHER
- **用法**：
  - further = 抽象程度
  - farther = 物理距离
- **例句**：
  - We need to discuss this **further**.
  - The store is **farther** than I thought.

### 22. WHOSE vs WHO'S
- **用法**：
  - whose = 谁的
  - who's = who is
- **例句**：
  - **Whose** phone is this?
  - **Who's** coming to the party?

### 23. ITS vs IT'S
- **用法**：
  - its = 它的
  - it's = it is
- **例句**：
  - The dog wagged **its** tail.
  - **It's** a beautiful day.

### 24. YOUR vs YOU'RE
- **用法**：
  - your = 你的
  - you're = you are
- **例句**：
  - Is this **your** bag?
  - **You're** doing great!

### 25. THEIR vs THERE vs THEY'RE
- **用法**：
  - their = 他们的
  - there = 那里
  - they're = they are
- **例句**：
  - **Their** car is parked **there**.
  - **They're** going to the park.
